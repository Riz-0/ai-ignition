# The 2am Shoppers  
  
**One app for thoughtful shopping, savings goals and personalised customer conversations.**  
  
**Hackathon:** Problem Statement 1 by Eslitec    
**Focus:** Personalised, automated e-commerce conversations that support retention and repeat purchases    
**Target audience:** Students, working adults and participating e-commerce businesses    
**Status:** Proposed app concept; marketplace integrations require validation  
  
## 1. The Idea in Simple Terms  
  
The 2am Shoppers brings shopping information into one place and helps users decide what to buy, when to revisit an item and when to reorder something they already like.  
  
At sign-up, users choose **Student**, **Working Adult** or **Business**. Student and working adult modes include a built-in savings and budgeting workspace. Business mode helps merchants market their own products, serve their existing customers and measure repeat purchases.  
  
The guiding rule is **help customers buy at a suitable time and within their chosen budget**. Retention comes from relevant service, useful products and trust. Saving goals and essential expenses take priority over promotional prompts.  
  
With permission, the app uses available purchase records, saved product links, searches inside the app and customer preferences. It sends casual push notifications that start a useful conversation rather than sending the same promotion to everyone.  
  
Users can respond, and the AI changes its recommendations, timing and follow-ups accordingly.  
  
**Example:** You save office shoes, set a budget under $40 and ask for a payday reminder. The app reminds you at your chosen time, checks available product information and suggests suitable options. If you say the style is wrong, it changes its suggestions.  
  
## 2. The Problem We Address  
  
Students and working adults may shop across several platforms, save products they later forget, repeatedly search for the same items and struggle to keep track of useful offers. Students may need a simple weekly view of allowance and expenses; working adults may prefer monthly planning around income, commitments and savings goals.  
  
Meanwhile, businesses already hold customer records and purchase history but may struggle to turn that information into relevant conversations. Generic promotions can miss the customer's actual needs, budget or readiness to buy.  
  
The 2am Shoppers connects these needs: customers receive useful shopping assistance, while participating businesses can provide personalised follow-ups and measure whether customers return and purchase again.

## 5. What the App Will Do  
  
### Step 1: Learn Your Preferences  
  
During onboarding, users choose:  
  
- Student, working adult or business mode, with the option to switch later.  
- Interests, such as fashion, coffee, beauty or workday essentials.  
- A general budget or a budget for a particular product.  
- Favourite shops and product preferences.  
- Notification frequency and quiet hours.  
- An optional reminder date, such as payday.  
- An optional weekly or monthly budget, savings goals and essential-expense reserves.  
  
Preferences can be changed at any time. The app should not infer someone's salary or payday from their age or shopping habits.  
  
### Step 2: Bring Shopping Information Together  
  
Possible inputs include:  
  
- Purchase records from supported, authorised connections.  
- Uploaded receipts or supported order exports.  
- Product links shared by the user.  
- Wishlists and searches within The 2am Shoppers.  
- Existing customer records supplied by a participating merchant through an authorised integration.  
  
The app cannot automatically read every marketplace's private search history. External search activity is used only when a supported integration makes it available with appropriate permission.  
  
### Step 3: Decide What Might Be Useful Next  
  
The app looks for relevant signals, such as:  
  
- A saved item the user wants to revisit.  
- Repeated interest in a category within the app.  
- A verified offer matching a chosen budget.  
- A recurring purchase that may need replenishment.  
- Feedback showing that the previous purchase did not suit the customer.  
  
It distinguishes confirmed preferences from estimates.  
  
### Step 4: Send a Casual Push Notification  
  
Illustrative notifications:  
  
> Still thinking about that bag? It's now within your $30 budget.  
> Coffee check — Running low, or still stocked?  
> You asked for a payday reminder — want to revisit your wishlist?  
> Buying snacks with your colleagues? There's an eligible group bundle to check out.  
  
Price, stock, discount and group-deal claims must come from verified product or merchant information.  
  
### Step 5: Adapt to Your Response  
  
| Response | What the app does |  
| --- | --- |  
| Interested | Show details, suitable options or the purchase link |  
| Too expensive | Offer eligible lower-cost alternatives or a user-chosen price alert |  
| Remind me later | Ask when and pause the current follow-up |  
| Wrong style | Update preferences and suggest different options |  
| Already bought | End that purchase prompt and offer optional after-purchase help |  
| Still have enough | Pause the replenishment journey |  
| Not interested | Stop this recommendation and adjust future suggestions |  
| Stop notifications | Honour the notification preference and end push follow-ups |  
  
### Step 6: Help You Take the Next Step  
  
The app can show product alternatives, explain differences, show verified offers, open purchase links and prepare a cart where supported. Users complete checkout. The app does not buy automatically.  
  
### Step 7: Follow Up After the Purchase  
  
Check satisfaction, route unresolved problems to support, offer replenishment for repeat-use products.

## 8. Three Interfaces: What Each Party Sees  
  
Student and working adult modes are customer profiles. Business mode is a separate merchant workspace. Switching views must not merge personal finances with merchant records.  
  
| Area | Student | Working adult | Business |  
| --- | --- | --- | --- |  
| Main question | What can I spend this week while saving for my goal? | How does this purchase fit my monthly plan? | Which customers should we engage, and what is the useful next action? |  
| Default planning period | Weekly | Monthly or custom pay cycle | Campaign period and customer cohort |  
| Savings | Small goal pockets, progress and optional missions | Planned allocations, multiple goals and summaries | No access to shoppers' private balances or goals |  
| Shopping | Budget-filtered wishlist and straightforward options | Planned purchases, replenishment and comparisons | Own catalogue, product campaigns and customer journeys |  
  
### Student Interface  
  
Navigation: Home · Discover · Save · Wishlist · Assistant  
  
| Screen | What it shows |  
| --- | --- |  
| Home | Weekly allowance/income, recorded expenses, essential reserves, remaining discretionary budget and one next-step card |  
| Discover | Products within the chosen cap, short demonstrations and clearly marked sponsored items |  
| Save | Goal pockets, target amounts, progress bars, contribution plan and optional badges |  
| Wishlist | Saved products, price timestamp, goal link and Wait / Save / Open listing actions |  
| Assistant | Quick reply buttons and short explanations |  
  
Illustrative weekly plan: $80 allowance − $45 reserved essentials − $15 savings = $20 discretionary.  
  
Gamification: Optional weekly check-ins, progress milestones, budget-learning missions and badges. Reward saving and reflection — not purchases or ad clicks. Users can turn gamification off.  
  
### Working Adult Interface  
  
Navigation: Overview · Spending · Savings · Shopping · Assistant  
  
| Screen | What it shows |  
| --- | --- |  
| Overview | Current pay cycle, recorded income, expense reserves, savings allocations, discretionary budget and upcoming planned purchases |  
| Spending | Categories, subscriptions, recurring expenses and source-labelled transactions |  
| Savings | Multiple goals, planned contributions, projected completion and editable allocation rules |  
| Shopping | Wishlist, comparisons, replenishment checks and price alerts |  
| Assistant | Brief tradeoffs, timing options and a clear next action |  
  
Illustrative monthly plan: $2,400 income − $1,500 essentials − $400 savings − $200 buffer = $300 discretionary.  
  
### Business Interface  
  
Navigation: Overview · Products · Customers · Journeys · Marketing · Inbox · Analytics · Connections  
  
Businesses see only their authorised customer context. The app does not reveal shoppers' private budgets, allowances or goal details to merchants.  
  
## 9. Built-in Savings Workspace  
  
Planning and tracking ledger only. Money remains in the user's existing accounts. Recording "$10 saved" does not transfer funds.  
  
Budget formula: Remaining discretionary = confirmed income − essential reserves − savings allocations − buffer − recorded discretionary spending.  
  
Label as a recorded plan with last-update time and missing inputs. Forecast progress is a projection, not a guaranteed outcome.  
  
### Savings-to-Shopping Process  
  
1. Save a product and confirm an estimated total.  
2. Choose "Save toward this" and create or link a goal pocket.  
3. Set a contribution plan and reminder preferences.  
4. Record contributions; planned contributions remain separate from completed entries.  
5. Pause purchase prompts if the item is outside the user's chosen budget.  
6. When the goal is funded in the ledger, recheck price and stock.  
7. User reviews the listing and completes checkout externally.  
8. Record the purchase and reconcile the linked goal.  
  
## 10. Connecting the Intended Shopping Apps  
  
Connections Hub for Shopee, SHEIN, TikTok/TikTok Shop, Taobao and Pinduoduo. Each platform card shows available actions and actual access level.  
  
Four integration routes:  
1. Share or paste a product link  
2. Import a purchase record  
3. Authorised merchant connection  
4. External purchase handoff  
  
Show Live connection / Imported records / Link-only / Needs attention / Demo data on each card. Never show "Connected" if only a product link was saved.  
  
## 11. Example End-to-End Journey  
  
Alicia (working adult) finds coffee in the discovery feed. She says "Too expensive." The agent offers a smaller pack. She asks for a reminder on the 25th. The agent checks stock before sending it. She completes checkout. The app records the purchase and later checks satisfaction.  
  
### Student Variant: Save Before Buying  
  
Student saves $60 shoes. Goal has $20 recorded. Dashboard shows contribution plan and pauses purchase prompts. When funded, app checks stock, offers link. If the student changes their mind, goal is repurposed and journey exits.  
  
### Working Adult Variant: Replan After an Expense  
  
Customer records an unexpected expense, reduces shopping cap. App pauses bundle prompt, offers to move reminder. At the new date, checks interest and stock before resuming.

## 13. How We Measure Success  
  
| Measure | What it tells us |  
| --- | --- |  
| Repeat-purchase rate | Share of eligible existing customers who buy again |  
| Retention | Share of a cohort who remain active purchasers |  
| Conversation-to-purchase rate | Share of engaged customers with a confirmed purchase |  
| Savings goal progress | Recorded contributions versus chosen targets |  
| Budget adherence | Recorded spending versus user-defined caps |  
| Budget-respecting engagement | Purchase prompts deferred by preference/budget rules |  
| Opt-outs and complaints | Whether engagement is becoming unwanted |

## 15. Short Pitch  
  
> The 2am Shoppers brings shopping, savings goals and personalised customer conversations into one app. Students get a simple, optionally gamified budgeting experience; working adults get practical spending and savings planning; businesses get tools to market their own products and support returning customers. The agent adapts reminders and recommendations to confirmed preferences and changing circumstances, while businesses measure repeat purchases and retention.
