The 2am Shoppers

An AI shopping companion that remembers your interests, follows your budget and adapts when you respond.

For working youths who shop across multiple platforms and forget what they saved. For businesses that need to turn customer data into relevant conversations that lead to repeat purchases.

01 · Problem

Shopping scattered. Follow-ups generic. Retention hard to measure.

Working youths shop across multiple platforms, save products they later forget and struggle to track useful offers. Businesses hold customer data but can't turn it into relevant conversations.

🛍️

Scattered Shopping

Products saved across Shopee, SHEIN, Taobao. Repeated searches for the same items. No way to track it all.

📢

Generic Promotions

Businesses send the same message to everyone. Doesn't match actual needs, budget or timing.

📊

Unmeasured Retention

Hard to tell whether outreach brings customers back. No clear link between engagement and repeat purchase.

What this looks like in practice

Customer view: You save office shoes on three different sites, forget about them, then search again two weeks later.

Business view: You know this customer bought coffee once. You send a generic "20% off" message. They don't respond. You can't tell why.

02 · Solution

One app. Personalized conversations. Adaptive journeys.

The 2am Shoppers brings shopping information together, remembers confirmed preferences and starts relevant conversations that adapt to customer responses.

Simple example

You save office shoes, set a budget under $40 and ask for a payday reminder.

→ The app reminds you at your chosen time, checks product info and suggests suitable options.

→ If you say the style is wrong, it changes its suggestions.

03 · Positioning

How this fits Eslitec — and our own direction

Eslitec focuses on AI CRM for SMBs. MantaGO supports conversational commerce, chatbots and connected messaging. The 2am Shoppers is a proposed customer-retention app in the same domain, designed for working youths.

Business outcomes Track retention and repeat purchases Show reason for engagement and verified result

What makes this personal to our team

The idea carries forward the strongest parts of our original scheduling agent:

  • Prioritisation: Decide which customer journey needs attention and why.
  • Preference-based planning: Fit reminders around dates and quiet hours the customer chooses.
  • Change recovery: Replan when price, stock, needs or purchase status changes.
  • Action with control: Execute allowed reminders while requiring approval for spending.
  • Visible outcomes: Show what the agent did and whether it led to another purchase.

"2am" is the brand personality, not a default notification time. Notifications respect each customer's quiet hours.

04 · Key Features

Two views: Customer and Business

👤

Customer View

  • Preferences and budget settings
  • Saved products and reminders
  • Personalized discovery feed (TikTok-inspired)
  • AI shopping conversation
  • Purchase history and reorder links
  • Notification and data controls
💼

Business View

  • Customer records and purchase history
  • Customer segments and active journeys
  • Reasons for proposed engagement
  • Conversation outcomes and support handovers
  • Campaign drafts and approval controls
  • Repeat-purchase and retention reporting

Customer engagement approaches

💬 Interest-based conversations

Ask what matters about a saved product → Understand intent and address hesitation

🎥 Personalized product content

Show demonstrations or comparisons → Help evaluate suitable options

📅 Customer-chosen payday reminders

Revisit an item on a selected date → Respect timing, encourage return visit

🤝 Colleague group buying

Offer approved coffee or snack bundles → Make social repeat shopping convenient

🔄 Smart replenishment

Ask whether a regular product is running low → Make repeat purchases easier

✅ After-purchase care

Ask about satisfaction, route problems to support → Address reasons customers leave

05 · Demo Journey

Example: Coffee replenishment for a returning customer

Customer: Alicia, a young working adult. Merchant: A fictional coffee and workday-snack shop.

1

Alicia previously bought coffee from the shop and agreed to relevant updates.

2

She searches for office coffee inside The 2am Shoppers.

3

The app shows a suitable product demonstration in its discovery feed.

4

Alicia responds: "Interested, but too expensive."

5

The agent checks the merchant's catalogue and offers a smaller pack or an eligible colleague bundle.

6

Alicia chooses: "Remind me on the 25th."

7

The agent schedules the reminder and pauses immediate follow-ups.

8

Before the reminder, it checks whether the product and offer are still available.

9

If something changed, it explains the change and offers suitable alternatives.

10

Alicia opens the listing, reviews the purchase and completes checkout.

11

The app records a verified purchase, ends the current prompt and later checks satisfaction.

Key point: This journey has a clear trigger, decisions, adaptations and exits. A product-link click alone is not treated as a completed purchase.
06 · MVP Scope

Hackathon demonstration scope

Build one complete journey for one fictional merchant and product category before expanding across marketplaces.

Primary demo

Turn a merchant's existing coffee customers into personalised conversations that lead to a tracked repeat order.

Show one customer selecting a later date, one choosing a lower-cost option and one requiring human support.

✅ Core Features (MVP)

  • Customer onboarding: interests, budget, notifications
  • Existing customer records + purchase-history import
  • Merchant onboarding: goals, offers, contact limits
  • Product catalogue, shared links, in-app search
  • Simple customer segmentation
  • AI conversation with response-based adaptation
  • Casual notifications and user-chosen reminders
  • One after-purchase or replenishment journey
  • Business dashboard with journey explanations
  • Human support handover and clear exits

🎬 Demonstration Scope

Use sample data to demonstrate 2–3 customers receiving different journeys.

Clearly label: Simulated purchases, feeds, notifications or external integrations.

Keep real push notifications or merchant integrations only where the build supports them.

🚀 Later Enhancements

  • Additional marketplace integrations
  • Real short-video content and ad publishing
  • Group-buy checkout
  • Multilingual conversations
  • Light/dark mode
  • Broader analytics and product comparisons

Business process (behind the scenes)

1. Merchant onboarding → goals, records, catalogue, limits
2. Check data quality → missing dates, duplicates, consent
3. Segment customers → assign relevant journeys
4. Select a trigger → first order, reminder date, replenishment, inactivity
5. Choose conversation → history + catalogue + intent + preferences
6. Start approved journey → send via implemented channel
7. Adapt and act → respond, remind later, recheck price/stock
8. Hand over when needed → escalate to staff, pause sales
9. Exit or follow up → after purchase, opt-out or limit
10. Measure outcomes → link orders to journeys, report vs holdout
07 · Success Metrics

How we measure retention and repeat purchase

🔄

Repeat-purchase rate

Share of existing customers who buy again within a defined period

📈

Retention

Share of a customer cohort who remain active purchasers

⏱️

Time to next purchase

Whether customers return sooner after useful engagement

💰

Customer lifetime value

Longer-term contribution; estimates must state assumptions

💬

Conversation-to-purchase

Share of engaged customers with confirmed purchase in attribution window

⚠️

Opt-outs & complaints

Whether engagement is becoming unwanted

For a real pilot

Compare eligible customers receiving the journey with a randomly assigned holdout group. Track repeat purchases from the participating business, because purchases elsewhere do not establish retention for that merchant.

Hackathon note: Sample data can demonstrate the reporting process. It does not prove a real increase in retention or customer lifetime value.
