01 · Problem

Scattered shopping. Generic promotions. No savings plan.

Students and working adults shop across multiple platforms, forget saved products and have no single view of budget versus spending. Businesses hold customer data but can't turn it into relevant conversations.

🛍️

No single view

Products scattered across Shopee, SHEIN, TikTok, Taobao and Pinduoduo with no unified budget view.

💸

No savings structure

Students need weekly allowance tracking. Working adults need monthly planning with savings goals alongside shopping.

📢

Generic promotions

Businesses send the same message to everyone. Doesn't match the customer's budget, timing or readiness to buy.

⚖️
The guiding rule

Help customers buy at a suitable time and within their chosen budget. Retention comes from relevant service, useful products and trust. Saving goals and essential expenses take priority over promotional prompts.

02 · Three Modes

One app, three tailored experiences

At sign-up you choose Student, Working Adult or Business. You can switch modes later. Switching views never merges personal finances with merchant records.

🎓

Student

What can I spend this week while saving for my goal?
Home · Discover · Save · Wishlist · Assistant

  • Save — Goal pockets, target amounts, progress bars, contribution plan and optional badges
  • Wishlist — Saved products, price timestamp, goal link, Wait / Save / Open actions
  • Assistant — Quick reply buttons and short explanations

Overview · Products · Customers · Journeys · Marketing · Inbox · Analytics · Connections
  • Overview — Active journeys, repeat customers, confirmed orders, support queue and suggested actions

03 · Savings Workspace

A planning ledger built into the app

The savings feature is a planning and tracking ledger. Money stays in the user's existing accounts. Recording "$10 saved" does not transfer funds, create a deposit or earn interest.

Budget formula
Remaining discretionary = confirmed income − essential reserves − savings allocations − buffer − recorded discretionary spending
Labelled as a recorded plan with last-update time and any missing inputs shown.
1

Save a product and confirm an estimated total including known delivery charges.

2

Choose "Save toward this" and create or link a goal pocket.

3

Set a contribution plan and reminder preferences.

4

Record contributions. Planned and completed entries remain separate.

5

Purchase prompts are paused while the item is outside the chosen budget.

6

When the goal is funded in the ledger, recheck price and stock, then offer the link.

7

User reviews the listing and completes checkout externally.

8

Record the purchase and reconcile the linked goal without double-counting.

04 · Connections Hub

Five platforms, four integration routes

One Connections Hub for Shopee, SHEIN, TikTok Shop, Taobao and Pinduoduo. Each platform card shows the actual access level — not just "connected."

1

Share or paste a link

Store the source, link and user-confirmed product details. Works for all five platforms immediately.

Link-only
2

Import a purchase record

Upload a receipt, screenshot or available order export. User confirms extracted details before they update spending.

Imported records

Authorised merchant connection

Apply for the relevant platform integration. Request only the scopes the service permits. Verify region and eligibility first.

Live connection
4

External purchase handoff

Open the original listing or checkout link. Track a confirmed order only through an authorised callback or clearly labelled user confirmation.

05 · Example Journeys

Three journeys, one platform

💼 Alicia (Working Adult) — Coffee replenishment

1

Alicia previously bought coffee and agreed to relevant updates.

2

She searches for office coffee inside The 2am Shoppers.

3

AI shows a product demo. Alicia replies: "Interested, but too expensive."

4

Agent checks catalogue and offers a smaller pack or colleague bundle.

5

Alicia: "Remind me on the 25th." Agent schedules reminder, pauses follow-ups.

6

Before the reminder, agent verifies price and stock. If changed, explains and offers alternatives.

7

Alicia opens the listing, completes checkout. App records purchase and later checks satisfaction.

🎓 Student — Save before buying

1

Student saves a $60 pair of shoes from a shared listing.

2

Goal pocket has $20 recorded. App shows contribution plan and pauses purchase prompts.

3

Student records an unexpected expense. App recalculates discretionary budget.

4

When goal is funded, app checks stock, offers purchase link. If mind changed, goal is repurposed and journey exits.

💼 Working Adult — Replan after unexpected expense

1

Customer planned a coffee bundle for their pay-cycle date.

2

They record an unexpected expense, reduce shopping cap. App pauses the bundle prompt.

3

App offers to move the reminder to a later date.

4

At the new date, checks interest and stock before resuming. If no longer needed, journey exits.

Key principle: A product-link click alone is not treated as a completed purchase. The app tracks confirmed orders only through authorised callbacks or clearly labelled user confirmations.
