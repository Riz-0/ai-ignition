Quick questions

Who is this really for?

Anyone who wants to shop with intention — students stretching an allowance and working adults planning around payday. No finance background needed.

Can it see my bank account?

No. You tell it your budget and goals. It never touches your bank, and shops never see your private numbers.

Will it spam me?

Never. You set quiet hours and how often it can message you. It only pings when it's genuinely useful.

Which shops does it work with?

Save links from Shopee, SHEIN, TikTok Shop, Taobao and Pinduoduo — all in one tidy list.
